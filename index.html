01 / ABOUT

Building, teaching,
and creating.

Kyle Gordon is a technology educator and IT professional with a background spanning mechanical engineering, computer science, education, digital media, and technology development.

His work centers on helping students and organizations understand technology not simply as a tool, but as a platform for creativity, problem-solving, entrepreneurship, and innovation.

With experience in education, technology, creative production, STEM, and digital design, Kyle brings an interdisciplinary approach to projects.

Outside of the classroom and professional work, his interests include music production, guitar, photography, video production, drones, RC technology, 3D design, 3D printing, and stargazing.
